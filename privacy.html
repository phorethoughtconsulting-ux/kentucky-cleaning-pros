Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Kentucky Cleaning Pros ("we," "us") is a residential cleaning company serving the Louisville, KY area. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it when you visit this site or book a cleaning.

What we collect

  • Information you give us — your name, phone number, and the details you enter when you book (preferred day and time, service type, and any notes you add).
  • We only ask for what we need to confirm your booking and schedule your cleaning. We do not collect payment details on this website.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request, confirm your booking, and schedule and provide your cleaning.
  • To contact you about your booking or appointment.
  • We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Who we share it with

Your booking details are processed on our behalf by our booking and scheduling platform (Tidy Track / HighLevel) so we can contact you about your cleaning. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone, and we do not share your SMS opt-in information or phone number with third parties for their own marketing.

Text messages (SMS)

If you opt in on our booking form, we may send you text messages about your booking — confirmations, scheduling, and reminders — and, only if you separately opt in, occasional offers. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. We do not share your SMS opt-in information or phone number with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Cookies & analytics

This site is a simple static website. It does not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

Keeping your information

We keep your request only as long as needed to serve you or as required by law. You may ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time — just contact us below.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change accordingly.
