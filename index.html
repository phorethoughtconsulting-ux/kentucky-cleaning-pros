Good to know

Questions, answered.

What's your satisfaction guarantee?

If you're not happy with a cleaning we did, tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and re-clean the missed areas free. If you're still not satisfied after that, we'll refund that service.

How do I book a cleaning?

Pick a day on the form above or give us a call. We personally review every booking and text you back to confirm the details — no spam, no pressure.

What's the difference between Standard, Premium, Deep / VIP, and Move-out?

Standard is the regular reset — floors, surfaces, kitchen and baths. Premium is standard elevated, with extra detail and high-touch finishes. Deep / VIP goes further — baseboards, appliances, and the corners a standard skips. Move-in / move-out is an empty-home clean, top to bottom, ready for keys or a deposit.

Are you insured?

Yes — Kentucky Cleaning Pros is fully insured. We're veteran-owned, and every job is done to a veteran's standard of care.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

It's entirely up to you. Many clients leave access instructions and come home to a finished house; others prefer to be there. Whatever's comfortable — just let us know when you book.

Do you work around pets?

Absolutely. Just mention your pets in the booking notes so the crew arrives prepared.

What areas do you serve?

Homes and apartments across Louisville and the surrounding neighborhoods. Not sure if we reach you? Ask — we probably do.

What if I need to reschedule?

Life happens. Just reach out and we'll find a new time that works for you.
